## Gate result

The package-license text and hash portion of M0-02 has evidence: the supplied archive contains English and controlling Arabic license PDFs, and each of the eight app OTF hashes matches a font in that archive. The live Arabic license page was checked on 2026-10-12. It permits app embedding only within a compiled/packaged/obfuscated product, prohibits redistribution, and also prohibits making the font independently extractable or reusable, including through apps. The project registers raw `.otf` resources with `UIAppFonts` (`Madar/App/Info.plist:36–47`); this source check does not establish that the shipped app package satisfies the no-extraction condition. This is an unresolved possible license/packaging conflict, not a legal conclusion.

The supplied archive's official-site download origin is also unverified; the embedded license requires downloads from that site. Keep the distribution gate open until the owner confirms the archive's origin and the publisher or an independently cleared font/packaging approach resolves the extraction condition. Do not treat the current design-token statement that app-bundle use is allowed as proof that both restrictions are met. No fonts, UI, license rule or distribution configuration changed; no legal conclusion or distribution approval is made here.
